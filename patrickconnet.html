Patrick Shop Business Manager — User guide

1. Create an account

Register with your business name, full name, email address and password. Use the same email to sign in later.

2. Add stock

Open Stock, enter product name, quantity, wholesale cost, selling price and low-stock alert level, then save.

3. Record a sale

Select a product, enter quantity, customer details, price and amount received. Stock is reduced and any remaining balance is recorded as a debt.

4. Collect a debt

Open Customer debts and select Receive payment. The balance will be updated.

5. Send a reminder

After three days, an unpaid balance is marked as due. Select WhatsApp or SMS to prepare a message. Automatic sending is a later setup stage.

6. Expenses and reports

Record expenses and use Reports to view sales, receipts, debts, gross profit and net profit.

7. Account security

Sign out when using a shared computer. Each account is restricted by database security rules to its own business records.
